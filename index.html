Selection methodology

NFL Classic roster: QB, 2 RB, 3 WR, TE, FLEX, DST (9 slots). CFB Classic uses QB, 2 RB, 3 WR, FLEX, SFLEX (8 slots). NHL Classic uses C, C, W, W, W, D, D, G, UTIL (9 slots). Confirm the selected DraftKings contest template before use.

PRIME ranks by imported projection, projection ceiling/uncertainty when supplied, salary efficiency, team implied total when available, ownership/leverage when supplied, and your selected objective. Vegas is a contextual modifier, not a replacement for projections.

LineStar account sign-in is not requested or stored. Use LineStar’s authorized CSV export until an official supported API connection is verified.

Privacy & limits

Player CSVs and projection files are read locally in this browser. This static app has no server, account, paid API, or cloud database. Browser storage can be cleared by iPadOS; keep your source CSVs as the durable copy.

Candidate rankings are not evidence of profitability. Verify player status, kickoff times, salary, roster eligibility, and current projections before entry.
